When to Visit India

Winter

Oct - Feb

Best for Rajasthan, Goa, Kerala, Delhi, Agra. Pleasant weather, perfect for sightseeing.

8°C - 25°C
  • Rajasthan Desert
  • Goa Beaches
  • Kerala Backwaters
  • Taj Mahal

Summer

Mar - Jun

Best for hill stations like Manali, Shimla, Darjeeling, Ladakh. Escape the heat.

15°C - 45°C
  • Manali & Shimla
  • Ladakh
  • Darjeeling
  • Uttarakhand

Monsoon

Jul - Sep

Best for Meghalaya, Coorg, Udaipur, Goa. Lush green landscapes and fewer crowds.

20°C - 35°C
  • Meghalaya
  • Coorg
  • Udaipur Lakes
  • Munnar
